Labels: NOT STARTED, IN PROGRESS, SOURCE COMPLETE, COMPILED, TESTED, VERIFIED. **No panel is COMPILED, TESTED or VERIFIED**: the sandbox has no Android SDK, NDK, Rust, Gradle download or network. "SOURCE COMPLETE" means: everything in the prompt that the engine can actually do is present in the source and wired to the real command. Things the engine cannot do are listed, not faked.
